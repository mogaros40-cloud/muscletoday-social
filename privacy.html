Privacy Policy — Muscle Today Uploader

Last updated: 2026-11-03

Muscle Today Uploader is an internal tool that uploads videos produced by Muscle Today (오늘근육) to Muscle Today's own social media accounts.

  • Data we use: only the access tokens and account IDs of our own accounts, needed to upload videos. We do not collect data from other users.
  • Storage: tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and are never shared or sold.
  • Retention: tokens are deleted when the connection is revoked or no longer needed.

The Muscle Today app's own privacy policy: muscletoday.web.app/en/privacy
